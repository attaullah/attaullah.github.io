Hi! I’m Attaullah, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at [The Shaikh Ayaz University, Shikarpur](https://saus.edu.pk). I’m a machine learning practitioner and researcher, and I teach undergraduate courses in artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning and data mining.

My background and history
======
I received my PhD from the [University of Waikato](https://waikato.ac.nz/), my MS from [Sukkur IBA University](https://iba-suk.edu.pk/), and my BS in Computer Science from the [National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences](https://nu.edu.pk/).

My research
======
My research investigates how unsupervised and self-supervised deep learning can improve supervised learning tasks when labelled data is scarce. In this semi-supervised setting, large amounts of unlabelled data are available alongside only a few labelled examples. My work includes self-training with Siamese networks, transfer learning from pretrained models, and combining self-supervision with self-training for image classification. See my [publications](/publications/) for details.
