In this tutorial, I introduced machine learning fundamentals and classification algorithms in detail, and demonstrated applying Naive Bayes, Decision Tree, and Random Forest algorithms using [WEKA](https://www.cs.waikato.ac.nz/ml/weka/) (Waikato Environment for KNowledge Analysis) tool on a simple dataset.
